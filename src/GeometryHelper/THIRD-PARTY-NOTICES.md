# Third-party notices

GeometryHelper holds code that comes from other projects. Their notices follow.

## Clipper2

The clipping engine of [Clipper2](https://github.com/AngusJohnson/Clipper2) 2.0.0, by Angus Johnson, from the C#
library of commit `f39457d` of 17 December 2025, changed for GeometryHelper: renamed, trimmed to what GeometryHelper
and its tests call, made internal, without nullable annotations, commented, and mended where it was found at fault or
slow. Among the changes for speed, the walk from an emptied output path up its owners to the path it was joined into
(`GetRealOutRec`) points each emptied path it passes straight at the next path it must not pass over, as the find of a
union-find does, and gives the same answers to the bit; Clipper2 has no such change up to commit `4be4655` of 28 October
2026. Building a polytree, the walk up the owners of each path to the one that holds it (`RecursiveCheckOwners`)
shortens the chains of emptied paths it passes in the same way, and the points of a path are found against a path of 64
points or more through that path's crossings with their line, sorted along it (`RingLines`); a path just made is not
looked for among the owners of the path that is to own it, nor, while every path is owned by an older one or none, any
path among the owners of an older one (`SetOwner`). These give the same answers to the bit too, and Clipper2 has none of
them up to the same commit.

- `src/GeometryHelper/Internal/Clipper`: the engine, compiled into GeometryHelper. The package carries its source too,
  embedded in the DLL's symbols, and this notice with it.
- `tests/GeometryHelper.UnitTest/Clipper`: Clipper2's C# tests, the utility that reads their cases, and the cases,
  moved to xUnit.

## ACadSharp

The DWG reader of GeometryHelper was written from the Open Design Specification for .dwg files, and where that leaves the
order or the meaning of the fields of an object open it follows [ACadSharp](https://github.com/DomCR/ACadSharp), by
Albert Domenech, as read at commit `c780699` of 10 November 2026. No file of ACadSharp is carried over; these files of
GeometryHelper decode the header variables and the objects of a DWG in the order ACadSharp's reader does, and are to be
taken as derived from it:

- `src/GeometryHelper/Dwg/DwgHeaderVariables.cs`: the header variables of AC1015 to AC1032, with their version
  conditions.
- `src/GeometryHelper/Dwg/DwgObjectReader.cs`, `DwgObjectReader.Entities.cs`, `DwgObjectReader.Tables.cs` and
  `DwgObjectReader.Objects.cs`: the common data of objects and entities, and the entities, table records and objects,
  with the fields of R2004 to R2018 (the colours, the owned objects, the string stream, the object head of R2010, the
  classes of R2004 and later).
- `src/GeometryHelper/Dwg/DwgContainer.R2004.cs` and `src/GeometryHelper/Dwg/DwgLz77.cs`: the container of AC1018 to
  AC1032 (the masked file header, the page map and the section map, the pages) and the decompression of its pages,
  whose handling of the codes 0x10 to 0x1F and of a first byte above 0x11 follows ACadSharp's.
- `src/GeometryHelper/Dwg/DwgObjectReader.DynamicBlocks.cs` and `DwgObjectWriter.DynamicBlocks.cs`: the order of the fields of
  the evaluation graph, the parameters (linear, visibility, flip, rotation, XY, polar, point, base point, lookup,
  alignment), the actions (stretch, polar stretch, move, scale, rotate, flip, array, lookup), the grips, the grip
  location component, the representation data and the purge preventer of a dynamic block (checked, and corrected where
  it differs, against the DXF AutoCAD 2021 writes of the same drawings).
